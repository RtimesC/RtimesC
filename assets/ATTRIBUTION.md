# Visual attribution

## Hero lettering

The hero uses **EMS Delight**, a single-line derivative of
[Delius](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Delius) by Natalia Raices. EMS
Delight was created by Sheldon B. Michaels, with SVG font conversion by
Windell H. Oskay, and is available from the
[SVG Fonts repository](https://gitlab.com/oskay/svg-fonts).

The generated profile asset expands the required glyphs into self-contained
SVG paths, then applies custom animation timing and colors. It has no external
font, script, or runtime dependency. The font-derived paths remain available
under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](./fonts/Delius-OFL.txt).

## Human–robot animation

“Man and robot with computers sitting together in workplace” is by
[Abdul Latif](https://lottiefiles.com/animoox) and was downloaded from its
[LottieFiles community page](https://lottiefiles.com/free-animation/man-and-robot-with-computers-sitting-together-in-workplace-QnbODCGAFt).
The page identifies it as free to use under the
[Lottie Simple License](https://lottiefiles.com/page/license).

The repository GIF is a locally rendered and optimized derivative of the
original vector animation. It is not offered as a standalone asset.
